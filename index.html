About OMICONS

OMICONS is a dedicated scientific icon library developed to support researchers, educators, students, healthcare professionals, and biotechnology organizations with high-quality, publication-ready scientific icons.

My collection covers Biology, Molecular Biology, Cell Biology, Genetics, Proteomics, Metabolomics, Bioinformatics, Laboratory Science, Clinical Diagnostics, and many other life science disciplines.

Every icon is carefully designed with scientific accuracy, consistency, and simplicity, making them suitable for research publications, conference presentations, teaching materials, websites, and scientific communication.



About the Founder

Hi, I'm Prashanth B J, the creator of OMICONS. I am a researcher with a passion for proteomics, metabolomics, bioinformatics, and computational biology. During my academic and research journey, I realized that finding professional scientific icons for presentations, publications, and teaching materials was often difficult and time-consuming.

OMICONS was created to solve this problem by providing a dedicated platform where the scientific community can access accurate, modern, and visually appealing icons designed specifically for life sciences.

My vision is to make OMICONS the world's leading scientific icon library, supporting students, researchers, educators, and professionals in communicating science more effectively.
