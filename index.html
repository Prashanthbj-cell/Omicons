About OMICONS

OMICONS is a scientific icon library created to support researchers, educators, students, healthcare professionals, and biotechnology organizations with high-quality, publication-ready scientific icons.

Our collection covers Biology, Molecular Biology, Cell Biology, Genetics, Proteomics, Metabolomics, Bioinformatics, Laboratory Science, Clinical Diagnostics, and other life science fields.

Each icon is designed with scientific accuracy, consistency, and simplicity, making them ideal for research publications, presentations, teaching materials, websites, and scientific communication.



About the Founder

Hi, I'm Prashanth B J, the creator of OMICONS. I am a researcher with interests in proteomics, metabolomics, bioinformatics, and computational biology.

During my research journey, I recognized the challenge of finding accurate and professional scientific icons for presentations, publications, and educational materials. OMICONS was created to provide a dedicated platform offering modern, visually appealing, and scientifically accurate icons for the life science community.

My vision is to build OMICONS into a global scientific icon library that helps students, researchers, educators, and professionals communicate science more effectively.

We are continuously expanding the OMICONS library. If you need a specific scientific icon that is not currently available, you can contact us with your requirements. We welcome suggestions and will create and add new icons to support the research and scientific community.
